# Lenses — eight windows, pick four

A lens is a way of looking, not a topic. Two lenses that would return the same situations are one
lens, and picking both wastes an agent and dilutes the report.

**Default dispatch: the first four.** Add a fifth or sixth only when the summary makes one of them
load-bearing — a regulator in the summary earns `對手反應`, a fixed date earns `時間與次序`. Six is
the ceiling; past six the returns overlap and the user is answering the same question in different
words.

| # | Lens | The question it keeps asking | Pick it when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `反面鋼人` | what does the smartest opponent say, and is he right? | always |
| 2 | `機制` | does the claimed mechanism actually hold? | the summary claims a causal chain, a price, a moat |
| 3 | `前提審計` | which premise is unstated, and which of them is testable? | always |
| 4 | `失敗模式` | what makes this fail in a way that cannot be undone? | always |
| 5 | `對手反應` | what does the strongest counterparty do once this is visible? | competitors, regulators, or a platform appear |
| 6 | `時間與次序` | what has to be true first, and what breaks if it is late? | there is a date, a runway, or a sequencing claim |
| 7 | `資源與替代` | who pays, in what currency, and what else that buys | the summary commits money, attention or a person |
| 8 | `規模與邊界` | what changes at 10× and what breaks below 1× | the idea claims to scale or to start small |

---

## 1. `反面鋼人`

- **Looks for:** the strongest version of the case against — not the objections the user already
  dismissed, the ones he has not met. Its situations are the moments where his answer would have to
  concede something.
- **Good situation:** 「最不同意你的人指著第一批客戶的來源說：那些人是你認識的，換一個不認識的人就不
  會買。你在什麼時候、用什麼方式遇到第一個不認識的買家？」
- **Fails as:** 「這個想法有風險」 — a mood, not a moment.

## 2. `機制`

- **Looks for:** the sentence in the summary that says *because*. Then asks whether the mechanism has
  ever held anywhere, at what cost, and what the cost curve looks like at the volumes claimed.
- **Good situation:** 「你寫的是『覆蓋率提高會壓低單位成本』。在覆蓋率從 10% 升到 40% 的過程中，哪一
  項成本會先上升？什麼時候你會看到它？」
- **Fails as:** questioning the mechanism in the abstract instead of naming the quantity in the
  summary that carries it.

## 3. `前提審計`

- **Looks for:** claims the summary asserts as background — a market size, a behaviour, a price, a
  permission — that nothing in the record establishes. Each becomes a situation where the user has to
  say how he would find out, or what he would do if it is false.
- **Good situation:** 「summary 把『監管會允許』當成前設。如果六個月後仍然沒有答覆，你的下一步是
  什麼？」
- **Fails as:** listing premises without turning them into a moment.

## 4. `失敗模式`

- **Looks for:** the irreversible failures. Not "it does not work" but "it does not work after the
  money is spent", "it works and then the counterparty owns the relationship", "it works and creates a
  liability that surfaces in year three".
- **Good situation:** 「第一批交付做完之後，客戶要求把合約主體換成他們的子公司。你會不會簽？」
- **Fails as:** 「失敗風險」 with no shape to the failure.

## 5. `對手反應`

- **Looks for:** what the strongest other party does once the idea becomes visible — cut price, copy
  it, block the channel, buy the supplier, complain to the regulator.
- **Good situation:** 「你公開報價之後，本地最大的同業把同款價格降到你的六成。你的回覆在幾日內要
  出現？」
- **Fails as:** assuming a reaction without naming who reacts and how it is observed.

## 6. `時間與次序`

- **Looks for:** ordering claims and deadlines. What must be true before the next step; what happens
  to the whole if step 2 slips by a quarter.
- **Good situation:** 「第一批交付延後三個月，這三個月的成本由誰承擔？」
- **Fails as:** generic schedule worry. The situation must name the date or the dependency.

## 7. `資源與替代`

- **Looks for:** the currency being spent — money, attention, a founder's hours, an option that closes
  — and what the same resource would buy elsewhere.
- **Good situation:** 「這件事會佔用你今年全部的可支配現金。同樣的錢放在原有業務上，年底的差別是
  什麼？」
- **Fails as:** 「資源不足」 without naming the resource or its alternative use.

## 8. `規模與邊界`

- **Looks for:** the discontinuity. What breaks at ten times the volume, and what stops working when
  there is only one customer. Both directions, because an idea can be wrong at either end.
- **Good situation:** 「如果明年客戶數變成十倍而你仍只有兩個人，哪一件事會最先做不下去？」
- **Fails as:** asking about scalability in general, which is the same question as 機制 and returns
  the same answer.

---

## Checking the dispatch before sending it

- **Two lenses, one window?** If you cannot state, in one sentence each, how two lenses would differ
  on this summary, drop one.
- **A lens with nothing to look at?** `機制` over a summary that contains no causal claim, or
  `對手反應` over a summary with no other party, returns an empty pass. That is allowed and useful —
  it tells the user the idea has no such exposure — but do not dispatch it as a substitute for a lens
  that does have material.
- **A lens the user asked for?** Honour it even if it duplicates; note the overlap in the report.

## After the returns arrive

Convergence is the strongest signal in this skill. When three lenses independently name the same
decision point, that point is not one lens's opinion — record it once, and record the count. The
report keeps the count visible: 「三個鏡頭都指到這裡」 is information the user cannot get from any
single view.
